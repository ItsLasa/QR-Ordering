Build a QR-code ordering web app.

USERS:
- Customer: scans a table QR, browses the menu, orders, tracks status.
  No account needed.
- Owner/Staff: manages menu, tables and incoming orders.

MVP FEATURES:
- Owner sign-up and login, shop profile (name,  logo, currency)
- Menu: categories, items (name, price, photo, description, available
  toggle), options/extras
- Tables with unique unguessable QR tokens, plus printable QR sheet (PDF)
- Public menu at /s/[slug]?t=[token]
- Cart and place order (with customer name, optional note)
- Live kitchen dashboard (new order sound, status buttons:
  New > Preparing > Ready > Completed)
- Customer order status page (live updates)
- Sold-out toggle per item
- Pay at counter (cash). Online payment comes later.
